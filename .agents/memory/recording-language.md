---
name: Unified recording language
description: Product terminology and mental model for personal activity recording and organisation sharing.
---

Use one recording model throughout My Impact: people record activities with Quick Log, and each saved activity contributes to their current impact record automatically. Regular or recurring activities are saved Quick Log shortcuts, not a separate logging flow. Photo capture is Quick Log with photo, not a separate product.

**Why:** Multiple labels and parallel flows made one basic action feel more complicated than it was and blurred personal recording with organisation submission and approval.

**How to apply:** Say “Record for yourself” for personal activity, “Review & share” when reusing a saved personal record for an explicit-submission organisation, and “Automatically shared” for consented logging. Reserve “organisation submission” and “organisation approval” for the organisation workflow. Never ask people to enter an already recorded activity again.

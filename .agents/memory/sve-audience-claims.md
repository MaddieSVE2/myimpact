---
name: SVE audience claims
description: Copy guidance for accurately describing who uses the Social Value Engine.
---

Describe the Social Value Engine as being used by a broad range of organisations. This includes public, voluntary, education, housing, charity, and private-sector organisations. Do not imply that its users are limited to local authorities, universities, housing associations, and charities.

The Social Value Engine is an accredited platform, accredited by Social Value International. State the accrediting organisation when making the claim. Do not describe it as “the UK's accredited platform,” which changes the meaning and implies unsupported national exclusivity.

**Why:** Narrow sector lists omit significant parts of SVE's user base, especially private-sector organisations. The accreditation is valid, but the wording must attribute it to Social Value International rather than imply UK-wide exclusivity.

**How to apply:** When writing or reviewing website copy, presentations, emails, metadata, or generated descriptions of SVE, use inclusive wording such as “used by a wide range of organisations across the public, private, and voluntary sectors.” Describe SVE as “accredited by Social Value International.”

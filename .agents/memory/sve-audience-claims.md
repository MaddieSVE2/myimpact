---
name: SVE audience claims
description: Copy guidance for accurately describing who uses the Social Value Engine.
---

Describe the Social Value Engine as being used by a broad range of organisations. This includes public, voluntary, education, housing, charity, and private-sector organisations. Do not imply that its users are limited to local authorities, universities, housing associations, and charities.

Do not call SVE “the UK's accredited platform” unless a current, authoritative source specifically supports both the accreditation and the exact scope of that wording.

**Why:** Narrow sector lists omit significant parts of SVE's user base, especially private-sector organisations, and unsupported accreditation wording can create a misleading factual claim.

**How to apply:** When writing or reviewing website copy, presentations, emails, metadata, or generated descriptions of SVE, use inclusive wording such as “used by a wide range of organisations across the public, private, and voluntary sectors.” Verify any quantified usage or accreditation claim before publishing it.
